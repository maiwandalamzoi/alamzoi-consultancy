Services

Each offer is scoped around a specific business or operational problem. Public prices are not invented; after an initial discussion, I can prepare a proposal for the agreed scope.

Offer 01

Agricultural data and GIS analysis

For farms, AgriTech teams, advisory organisations, NGOs, researchers and public-sector teams that need agricultural or spatial data turned into usable evidence.

Problem addressed

Field, crop, weather, sensor or GIS data exists, but it is messy, scattered, difficult to interpret or not yet useful for decisions.

Client receives

Depending on scope: cleaned datasets, field or crop maps, spatial analysis, dashboard views and a short findings report.

Information needed

Available datasets, area of interest, crop or system context, decision question, preferred output format and deadline.

Offer 02

Precision agriculture and digital agriculture advisory

For organisations considering satellite monitoring, sensors, farm software, advisory tools, digital records or precision-agriculture pilots.

Problem addressed

The organisation wants to use digital agriculture but needs a practical assessment of what is useful, affordable and realistic for its context.

Client receives

Depending on scope: needs assessment, technology selection, pilot design, implementation roadmap and practical training.

Information needed

Current workflow, users, farm or programme scale, existing tools, constraints, data availability, budget range if known and training needs.

Offer 03

Agricultural engineering and feasibility studies

For agribusinesses, development programmes, engineering teams and farm-technology projects that need technical assessment within demonstrated agricultural engineering expertise.

Problem addressed

A machinery, process, technology or implementation option needs to be compared before investment, procurement or pilot deployment.

Client receives

Depending on scope: technical assessment, equipment or process comparison, feasibility report, assumptions and implementation considerations.

Information needed

Objective, site or operating context, candidate technologies, technical constraints, available measurements, intended users and decision deadline.
