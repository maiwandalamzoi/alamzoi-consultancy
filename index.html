Core services

Five practical service offers.

Each assignment starts with the problem, available data, decision context and deliverables. Technical tools are selected after the scope is clear.

A. Geospatial analysis and remote sensing

Turn spatial and satellite data into usable evidence.

Client problem

You need to understand land use, vegetation, field conditions, spatial change or differences between areas.

What I can deliver

Satellite and GIS data processing, land-use and vegetation analysis, spatial comparisons, maps, documented datasets, reproducible analysis and a findings report.

Relevant example

Crop-stress and remote-sensing analysis, plus ZaminAI as a digital agriculture product example.

Secondary stack: GIS, QGIS, Python, Sentinel/Copernicus, Google Earth Engine and PostGIS where appropriate.

C. Applied AI and decision-support tools

Client problem

You have data and a decision question, but need to know whether modelling, classification, forecasting or an AI-assisted interface is useful.

What I can deliver

Problem assessment, baseline models, evaluated prototypes, dashboards, APIs or practical interfaces with documented limitations and handover.
