FLAGSHIP PLATFORM — LIVE MVP

ZaminAI

A full-stack AI platform, designed and deployed solo, turning live Sentinel-2 satellite data into field-level NDVI, water-stress and crop analytics for Afghan smallholder farmers — with a multilingual advisory assistant answering farming questions in English, Dari and Pashto.

Built with the needs of the 500M+ smallholder farmers worldwide who currently lack this kind of digital advisory support in mind — ZaminAI is a live, publicly explorable proof of what that support could look like, not a claim of current mass adoption.
