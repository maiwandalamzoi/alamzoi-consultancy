    Method demonstration

    Agricultural land suitability

    Screen a defined area against crop requirements using terrain, soil, climate and land-cover evidence.

    Open methodology
    Project details
    Study area
    User-selected field or planning boundary; no fixed client site is implied.
    Data sources
    Copernicus DEM; ISRIC SoilGrids; ERA5-Land; CORINE Land Cover. Data resolution, access conditions and licence must be checked for each assignment.
    Methodology
    Agree crop-specific criteria, screen constraints, derive terrain and slope, summarize available soil/climate layers, then document uncertainty. No score is calculated on this page.
    Deliverables
    Criteria matrix, constraint layers, suitability map and reproducible methods note.
    Limitations
    Regional rasters cannot replace field soil sampling, local agronomic advice or a site survey.
    Request a similar assessment

    Method demonstration

    Municipal green-space monitoring

    Explore repeatable vegetation monitoring for a Dutch municipality using comparable seasonal observations.

    Open methodology
    Project details
    Study area
    Nijmegen, Netherlands, shown as a named study context; this page does not process municipality imagery.
    Data sources
    Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A; PDOK administrative boundaries; optional KNMI weather observations for context.
    Methodology
    Compare cloud-screened NDVI within matched seasonal windows, stratify by land cover, and inspect vegetation change alongside acquisition dates and valid-pixel coverage.
    Deliverables
    Boundary and date specification, NDVI maps, change workflow, accessible summary and data-quality note.
    Limitations
    Illustrative workflow only: no satellite raster has been processed for this card. NDVI alone does not establish plant health or causality.
    Request a similar assessment

    Method demonstration

    Engineering & environmental site screening

    Identify spatial factors that should be reviewed before detailed engineering or environmental investigation.

    Open methodology
    Project details
    Study area
    A proposed site boundary supplied for a scoped desktop assessment.
    Data sources
    Copernicus DEM; CORINE Land Cover; European Environment Agency Natura 2000 data; relevant national open-data portals where available.
    Methodology
    Derive elevation and slope, overlay land-cover and designated-area constraints, record source date/scale, and flag items needing professional follow-up.
    Deliverables
    Terrain and slope maps, constraint layers, GIS package and preliminary screening note.
    Limitations
    Desktop GIS screening is preliminary and does not replace certified engineering, ecological, geotechnical or regulatory investigations.
